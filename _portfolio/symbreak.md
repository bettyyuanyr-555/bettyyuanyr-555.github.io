SymBreak focuses on hyperacute ischemic stroke detection from non-contrast CT, especially weak signals that are difficult to identify visually.

The project uses a false-belief implantation paradigm to generate bilaterally symmetric counterfactual cases from unilateral ischemic samples. I contributed to patient-level Symmetry-Breaking Index analysis, robustness evaluation across public datasets and held-out cohorts, and the analysis of response and error patterns.

**Status:** Manuscript under review at ICLR 2027.

**Mentors:** Shana D. Stites (UPenn), Yifang Ma (SUSTech), Lili Miao (The Chinese University of Hong Kong), Longxi Zhou (SUSTech)
