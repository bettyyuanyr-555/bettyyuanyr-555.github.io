SymBreak focuses on hyperacute ischemic stroke detection from non-contrast CT, especially weak signals that are difficult to identify visually. The project asks how statistical structure and carefully designed AI models can expose clinically meaningful signals while making model behavior easier to evaluate and interpret.

The project uses a false-belief implantation paradigm to generate bilaterally symmetric counterfactual cases from unilateral ischemic samples. I contributed to patient-level Symmetry-Breaking Index analysis, robustness evaluation across public datasets and held-out cohorts, and statistical analysis of response and error patterns. This work connects counterfactual data construction, statistical diagnostics, and trustworthy AI for medical imaging.

**Status:** Manuscript under review at ICLR 2027.
