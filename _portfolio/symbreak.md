SymBreak focuses on counterfactual symmetry-breaking evaluation for hyperacute ischemic stroke detection from non-contrast CT, especially weak signals that are difficult to identify visually. The project asks how statistical structure and carefully designed AI models can expose meaningful signals while making model behavior easier to evaluate and interpret.

The team method uses a false-belief implantation paradigm to generate bilaterally symmetric counterfactual cases from unilateral ischemic samples. I contributed to patient-level Symmetry-Breaking Index analysis, robustness evaluation across five public NCCT datasets, four held-out acute ischemic stroke cohorts, and multiple segmentation architectures, together with response and error-pattern statistics. These are model-evaluation results, not claims of clinical deployment.

**Status:** Manuscript under review at ICLR 2027.
