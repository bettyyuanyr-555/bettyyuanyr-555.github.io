During a summer internship at OPPO, I studied disagreement between objective video-quality measurements and expert severity ratings under latent scene structure and heterogeneous score distributions. The work provided method-transfer experience in measurement validity: understanding when an automated metric fails to represent the construct that experts actually judge.

I designed score-debiasing corrections and unsupervised scene clustering, and trained CNN/Transformer models to recover expert judgments from video features. By combining statistical correction, structured grouping of scene characteristics, and modern AI models, the work improved objective-subjective consistency from 82.2% to 94.6% for downstream imaging-algorithm evaluation.
