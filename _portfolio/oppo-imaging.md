During a summer internship at OPPO, I studied disagreement between objective video-quality measurements and expert severity ratings under latent scene structure and heterogeneous score distributions.

I designed score-debiasing corrections and unsupervised scene clustering, and trained CNN/Transformer models to recover expert judgments from video features. The work improved objective-subjective consistency from 82.2% to 94.6% for downstream imaging-algorithm evaluation.
