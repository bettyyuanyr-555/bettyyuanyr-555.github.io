As a remote research assistant at the University of Pennsylvania, I contributed to a systematic review of 28 HIV studies and an evidence map spanning HIV and Alzheimer's disease literatures.

I built a PubMed retrieval pipeline comparing dense, BM25, and hybrid retrieval with Recall@k, MRR, MAP@k, and NDCG. The goal is to make literature synthesis auditable by grounding study-level labels in citation-level evidence.

**Recognition:** Conference posters at AAIC Neuro Next 2026 and the Penn CFAR Scientific Symposium 2026.
