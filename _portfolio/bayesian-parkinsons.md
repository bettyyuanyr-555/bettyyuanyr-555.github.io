This project develops a leakage-safe 3D T1-weighted MRI pipeline for baseline de novo Parkinson's disease versus healthy control analysis. It is motivated by a central trustworthy-AI question: how can statistical knowledge about uncertainty, prevalence, and biological asymmetry improve the reliability of medical predictions?

The approach combines shared-weight bilateral representation learning, an HC-only symmetry constraint, and an explicit target-prevalence prior for class-conditional Bayesian posterior risk. Planned evaluation includes calibration, bootstrap uncertainty, site stability, and sensitivity analyses, bringing together Bayesian inference, representation learning, and statistical validation for more interpretable clinical AI.

**Status:** Exploratory methodological study.
