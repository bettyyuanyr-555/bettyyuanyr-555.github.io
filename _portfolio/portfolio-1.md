This project develops a statistical framework for studying how scientific discoveries emerge, concentrate, and diffuse across institutional and geographic knowledge networks. It uses large-scale bibliometric data to connect statistical inference with network structure and the science of science.

I built discovery-level OpenAlex corpora with entity disambiguation and non-research filtering, detected discovery-specific burst years, and identified foundation papers through citation-neighborhood expansion and hybrid language-model/citation ranking. The analysis combines network statistics, statistical learning, and AI-assisted information extraction to characterize heavy-tailed knowledge diffusion and identify the mechanisms through which ideas travel across research communities.

**Status:** Working paper in progress.
